Bluprynt Proof, at [compliance.bluprynt.com](https://compliance.bluprynt.com), is the Compliance Hub for regulators, exchanges and other reviewers. You publish forms, review what issuers submit through [Bluprynt Passport](/passport/overview), and issue credentials that anyone can check.

<Info>
Bluprynt Proof is in early access. Ask [product@bluprynt.com](mailto:product@bluprynt.com) for an organization.
</Info>

## What you can do

| Area | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | See submitted, issued and rejected applications and recent work. |
| **Applications** | Accept, review and decide on issuer applications. |
| **Registry** | Browse organizations, assets and credentials in the shared record. |
| **Credentials** | Define the credentials your organization issues, such as KYI. |
| **Form manager** | Build the forms issuers fill in to apply for a credential. |
