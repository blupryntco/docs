## Accounts and access

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I create my own account?">
    No. An Organization Admin or Super Admin invites you from **Users**. See [Members and settings](/proof/members).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the Compliance Hub the same login as Bluprynt Passport?">
    No. [Bluprynt Passport](/passport/overview) is for issuers at app.bluprynt.com. The Compliance Hub is for supervisors at compliance.bluprynt.com.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a member belong to two organizations?">
    Each member belongs to one organization in the Compliance Hub.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I change my email or password?">
    Click your avatar, then **Profile**, to open **Account settings**. See [Members and settings](/proof/members#your-account-settings).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I switch to dark mode?">
    Yes. Click your avatar, then **Toggle theme**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Watchlist and credentials

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the Watchlist the same as the Registry?">
    Yes. The Compliance Hub renamed **Registry** to **Watchlist**. Links under `/registry` still work.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Monitoring the same as the Watchlist?">
    No. **Monitoring** in the sidebar isn't available yet. The Watchlist is.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between a credential and an attestation?">
    A credential is a kind of attestation: one your organization issues about a regulated status, such as a licence.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
