Every Bluprynt Proof page uses these terms. Learn them once here; the feature pages link back. Terms shared by all Bluprynt products are in [Concepts](/concepts).

## Your organization

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Organization** | Your regulator, supervisor or firm. Every member, form, application and credential belongs to it. |
| **Sector** | **Public sector** (regulators, supervisors, central banks) or **Private sector** (exchanges, custodians, underwriters, rating agencies, investors). Picked once, when the organization is created. It changes the dashboard's wording, not the features. |
| **Member** | A person in your organization, with one role |

## Onboarding and due diligence

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Form** | Questions you publish for issuers to answer |
| **Application** | One issuer's answers to one form |
| **Form type** | **Application** needs your review before a credential is issued. **Filing** issues it on submit. |
| **Information request** | A form you send to a specific issuer from the dashboard, the Watchlist or an asset page |
| **Credential** | Your on-chain statement about an issuer or asset, such as a licence or a listing approval. A kind of attestation. |

## Ongoing monitoring

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Watchlist** | The issuers, assets and credentials you monitor. Formerly *Registry*. |
| **Resolved data** | Facts the Compliance Hub reads for you from chain and from Bluprynt's data sources, such as supply, markets and admin controls |
| **Requested data** | Facts only the issuer can give, such as reserves and legal terms. Blank until the issuer files. |
| **Asset graph** | What an asset depends on: collateral, yield sources, oracles and controllers. See [Asset Dependency Engine](/tools/asset-dependency-engine). |
| **Disclosure alignment** | Where what the issuer declared differs from what's observed on-chain, such as the backing ratio |
