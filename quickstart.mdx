<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Issue a token" icon="coins">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create your organization">
        Sign up at [app.bluprynt.com](https://app.bluprynt.com) and set up your organization. See [Set up your organization](/passport/organization).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify your asset with KYI">
        Paste the token address, confirm what Bluprynt reads from chain, and sign with the wallets that control the token. See [Verify an asset](/passport/verify-asset).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Publish Proof of Collateral">
        Add references for what backs the token and submit the draft Bluprynt prepares. See [Publish Proof of Collateral](/passport/proof-of-collateral).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Verify or supervise" icon="scale">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign in to Bluprynt Proof">
        Bluprynt Proof is in early access. Ask [product@bluprynt.com](mailto:product@bluprynt.com) for an organization, then sign in at [compliance.bluprynt.com](https://compliance.bluprynt.com).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add the assets you oversee">
        Add an asset by token address, or add the issuer behind it. See [Watchlist](/proof/registry).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Request and review information">
        Send a form to an issuer and review what comes back. See [Applications and review](/proof/applications).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="I'm researching compliance information" icon="search">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Search for an asset">
        Open [explorer.bluprynt.com](https://explorer.bluprynt.com) and search by name, ticker, issuer or contract address. You don't need an account. See [Search and browse](/explorer/search).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Read the asset page">
        Check the issuer, status tags, listed venues and chain deployments, then move through the tabs. See [The asset page](/explorer/asset-page).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check what is disclosed">
        The disclosure checklist shows which facts are on record and which are **Not disclosed**. Opening source documents needs Explorer Pro. See [Disclosure checklist](/explorer/disclosure-checklist) and [Access and provenance](/explorer/access-and-provenance).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Monitor changes">
        With Explorer Pro, add the asset to your watchlist to get classified disclosure changes. See [Disclosure Monitoring](/tools/disclosure-monitoring).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Read it from your systems">
        Pull your watchlist, changes and notifications with an Explorer API key. See [Explorer API](/api/explorer/overview).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Build with the API" icon="terminal">
    Check a badge with no key at all:

    ```bash
    curl -s -o kyi.svg -w "%{http_code} %{content_type}\n" \
      "https://integrations.bluprynt.com/api/v1/badges/kyi_verified?variant=full"
    # 200 image/svg+xml
    ```

    Then choose an integration, or compare all three in [Build with Bluprynt](/developers/overview):

  </Tab>
