<Tabs>
  <Tab title="I issue a token" icon="coins">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create your organization">
        Sign up at [app.bluprynt.com](https://app.bluprynt.com) and set up your organization. See [Set up your organization](/passport/organization).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Verify your asset with KYI">
        Paste the token address, confirm what Bluprynt reads from chain, and sign with the wallets that control the token. See [Verify an asset](/passport/verify-asset).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Publish Proof of Collateral">
        Add references for what backs the token and submit the draft Bluprynt prepares. See [Publish Proof of Collateral](/passport/proof-of-collateral).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="I verify or supervise" icon="scale">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign in to Bluprynt Proof">
        Bluprynt Proof is in early access. Ask [product@bluprynt.com](mailto:product@bluprynt.com) for an organization, then sign in at [compliance.bluprynt.com](https://compliance.bluprynt.com).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add the assets you oversee">
        Add an asset by token address, or add the issuer behind it. See [Registry](/proof/registry).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Request and review information">
        Send a form to an issuer and review what comes back. See [Applications and review](/proof/applications).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="I'm a developer" icon="terminal">
    Check a badge with no key at all:

    ```bash
    curl -s -o kyi.svg -w "%{http_code} %{content_type}\n" \
      "https://integrations.bluprynt.com/api/v1/badges/kyi_verified?variant=full"
    # 200 image/svg+xml
    ```

  </Tab>
