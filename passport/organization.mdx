---
title: "Set up your organization"
description: "Create an organization in Bluprynt Passport, complete KYB and invite members."
---

KYI is issued to an organization, not to a person. Create one before you verify any asset.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to [app.bluprynt.com](https://app.bluprynt.com) and sign in, or choose **Sign Up** to create a login.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the organization">
    Open **Settings** → **Organizations** and click **Create organization**. One login can belong to several organizations, each with its own role.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/live/passport-organizations.png" alt="The Organizations tab in Passport settings with the Create organization button." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete KYB">
    Provide your legal entity details. When the check passes, the overview shows **Entity verified · KYB** and **My Entity** shows the entity of record.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/live/passport-entity.png" alt="My Entity page showing a KYB verified entity and the assets it issues." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite members">
    Click **Invite members** at the bottom of the sidebar, then enter each person's email and role.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Related companies only need their own organization if they hold assets themselves.
</Tip>
