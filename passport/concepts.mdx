Every Bluprynt Passport page uses these terms. Learn them once here; the feature pages link back. Terms shared by all Bluprynt products are in [Concepts](/concepts).

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Organization** | The issuer you act for. Assets, members, documents and reports belong to an organization. |
| **Member** | A person with access to an organization, with the Admin or Member role. |
| **KYB** | Verification of the organization's legal entity. It unlocks asset verification. |
| **Entity profile** | The registry-backed record of your legal entity: name, LEI, jurisdiction, licenses and responsible persons. |
| **KYI** | Know Your Issuer. The per-asset check that ties a token to your verified entity, closed by authority wallet signatures. |
| **Proof of Collateral** | The asset disclosure form that evidences what backs an asset. It needs KYI first. |
| **Trust center** | The asset page in Passport that shows each verification step and its state. |
| **Public page** | The read-only page at `verified.bluprynt.com` that anyone can open for an asset. |
| **Smart Disclosure** | A regulatory document, such as a MiCA white paper, that you prepare in **Documents**. |
