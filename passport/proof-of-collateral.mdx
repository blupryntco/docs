---
title: "Publish Proof of Collateral"
description: "Disclose what backs a verified asset on its public page."
---

[Proof of Collateral](/tools/proof-of-collateral) shows what backs a token: the reserve composition, the collateral ratio, how redemption works and the documents behind it. You publish it through the asset disclosure form on a KYI-verified asset.

<Frame caption="Product design: a verified asset with KYI and Proof of Collateral.">
  <img src="/images/design/passport-trust-center-verified.png" alt="Design of an asset page showing KYI Verified and Proof of Collateral." />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    In **My Assets**, open the asset. On the **Proof of Collateral** card, click **Start form**.
    <Frame caption="Live: the Proof of Collateral card on a verified asset.">
      <img src="/images/live/passport-asset-verified.png" alt="Passport asset page with the Proof of Collateral card and its Start form button." />
    </Frame>
    <Note>If Passport can't read the collateral on-chain, the card says so. The form doesn't depend on that read, so you can still open it.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the reserve">
    Enter the collateral type, each reserve asset with its amount, the current collateral ratio and the redemption process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach evidence">
    Upload attestation reports, custodian statements and constitutional documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and submit">
    Check the summary and submit. Once approved, the disclosure appears on the asset's public page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Drafts and disclosures under review never appear publicly. Only the approved disclosure is shown.
</Info>
