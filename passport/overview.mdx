Bluprynt Passport, at [app.bluprynt.com](https://app.bluprynt.com), is where an issuer proves who it is and what backs its tokens. What you publish here appears on each asset's public page and in the [Public API](/api/public/overview).

## What you can do

| Area | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | See every asset and what each one still needs. Start verifying a new asset. |
| **My Entity** | Check the legal entity of record and its KYB status. |
| **My Assets** | Open an asset's trust center: KYI status, Proof of Collateral and composition. |
| **Integrations** | Connect AML screening and smart-contract monitoring providers. |
| **Reports** | Generate entity and asset reports once their prerequisites are met. |
| **Documents** | Draft and publish disclosures such as MiCA white papers. |
| **Wallets** · **Attestations** | Review verified wallets and published KYI attestations. |
| **Analyze** | Check a white paper against MiCA. |
