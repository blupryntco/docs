Passport has two tools for regulatory documents. **Documents** drafts and publishes Smart Disclosures such as a MiCA registration. **Analyze** checks a white paper you already have against MiCA.

## Draft a Smart Disclosure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a document">
    Open **Documents** and click **Create New Document**.
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/live/passport-documents.png" alt="The Documents page with Create New Document and recent documents." />
    </Frame>
    <Note>Creating documents needs an active Bluprynt subscription. Passport prompts you to upgrade if your organization doesn't have one.</Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Answer each section">
    The form shows the questions for your jurisdiction and asset type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Publish the finished document. You can unpublish it later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check a white paper against MiCA

Open **Analyze**, click **Analyze New Document** and upload the white paper. Bluprynt flags missing or weak sections and gives you a report to download and share with your advisors.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/live/passport-analyze.png" alt="The Analyze page listing previous document analyses." />
</Frame>

<Warning>
The analysis is AI-assisted and doesn't replace legal advice. Before it runs, you confirm that final responsibility for compliance stays with you.
</Warning>

To classify a token under MiCA without signing in, use the free [MiCA checker](https://explorer.bluprynt.com/mica-checker) in Explorer.
