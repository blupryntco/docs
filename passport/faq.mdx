## Accounts and organizations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can one account belong to several organizations?">
    Yes. Each organization keeps its own assets, members and documents. Switch between them from the sidebar. **Settings → Organizations** lists every organization you belong to and your role in each.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my email later?">
    Yes. Open **Settings → Account** and edit **Email**. Bluprynt emails you to verify the change.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do we have to accept terms to create an account?">
    Yes. When you create an account, you agree to the [Terms of Service](https://bluprynt.com/terms) and the [Privacy Policy](https://bluprynt.com/privacy). If your legal team must review them, send them the links before you sign up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who owns the organization?">
    The member who creates it. Only the owner can delete the organization. Admins update the organization and manage its members. See [Members and roles](/passport/members).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the same login used for Bluprynt Proof?">
    Bluprynt Proof has its own sign-in at compliance.bluprynt.com. See [Bluprynt Proof](/proof/overview).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
