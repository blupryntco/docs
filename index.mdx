Bluprynt is the trust layer for tokenized assets. Issuers publish verified facts about who they are and what backs their tokens. Exchanges, custodians and supervisors read the same record.

## Products

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Bluprynt Passport" img="/images/cards/passport.webp" icon="badge-check" href="/passport/overview">
    For issuers. Verify your organization and assets with KYI, publish Proof of Collateral and manage disclosures.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bluprynt Proof" img="/images/cards/proof.webp" icon="shield-check" href="/proof/overview">
    For compliance teams at supervisors and regulated firms. Onboard and run due diligence on digital assets, then monitor their dependencies, disclosures and on-chain activity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Compliance Explorer" img="/images/cards/explorer.webp" icon="search" href="/explorer/overview">
    For researchers and analysts. Search the public registry of assets, issuers and disclosures, and monitor changes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
