## Access and data

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an account to search?">
    No. Search and every public record field are open to anyone.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is public data free to use?">
    Public record fields are open to view without an account. For programmatic access, use the [Explorer API](/api/explorer/overview).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why are source documents gated when the sources are public?">
    The gate covers the documents Bluprynt has gathered, the verbatim quotes taken from them, collateral and capital-flow analysis, and the complete disclosure record. The **Source:** links on public fields still point to the original pages.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I search from my own systems?">
    No. Search and the registry records serve the Explorer website only. The [Explorer API](/api/explorer/overview) covers tokenomics changes and your own watchlist, monitoring changes and notifications. For other programmatic access, ask [product@bluprynt.com](mailto:product@bluprynt.com).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Reading a record

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the disclosure percentage a risk score?">
    No. It counts how many checklist items are on record. It says nothing about whether the asset is safe.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does 'Not disclosed' mean the issuer is hiding something?">
    No. It means the registry holds no evidenced value. The issuer may publish it somewhere the registry hasn't read yet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Bluprynt fill a missing item with an estimate?">
    No. Items count only when a source supports them. Missing items stay missing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Issuers

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can an issuer correct its own record?">
    Yes. It clicks **Claim this profile** and completes [KYI](/tools/kyi) in the widget on the asset page: it verifies its business (KYB) and the asset. The record then shows **KYI Verified**, and the issuer finishes setting up its dashboard in [Bluprynt Passport](/passport/overview) to publish disclosures.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens after an issuer claims its profile?">
    The issuer verifies its business (KYB) and the asset in the KYI widget, and the record shows **KYI Verified**. Bluprynt then emails the issuer a link to [Bluprynt Passport](/passport/overview), where the issuer sets a password and lands on its verified asset. If the issuer already has a Passport account with that email, the asset is added to it and the email links to sign-in. Where the widget isn't available, the claim goes to Passport instead: sign in or create an account → verify your business (KYB) → [verify the asset](/passport/verify-asset), which Passport fills in from the Explorer page you came from.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
