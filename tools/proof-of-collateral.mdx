---
title: "Proof of Collateral"
description: "A machine-readable credential that shows what backs a tokenized asset."
---

An audit is a point-in-time PDF for one counterparty. Proof of Collateral is published once and read by many: lenders, exchanges, custodians, insurers and supervisors all check the same credential.

## What it covers

| Layer | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Reserve** | What backs the token, in what amounts, and the collateral ratio. |
| **Custody** | Where the reserve is held and how it's segregated. |
| **Redemption** | Who can redeem, how and on what timeline. |
| **Documents** | Attestation reports, custodian statements and constitutional documents. |

## Publish or read it

- **Issuers** publish it from a verified asset in [Bluprynt Passport](/passport/proof-of-collateral).
- **Readers** see it on the asset's page in [Compliance Explorer](/tools/compliance-explorer), and can embed the [Proof of Collateral badge](/api/public/badge-recipe).
