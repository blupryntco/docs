Know Your Issuer (KYI) binds a token to the verified organization that issued it. Exchanges, custodians and wallets read the same credential, so nobody relies on screenshots or self-reported claims.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/live/kyi.png" alt="The Know Your Issuer page on Bluprynt Explorer." />
</Frame>

## How it works

1. The issuer passes a **KYB** check on its legal entity.
2. Bluprynt finds the token on-chain and reads the wallets with authority over it.
3. The issuer signs with each wallet to prove control.
4. The asset gets the **KYI Verified** credential, and its public record shows the verified issuer.

## What it protects against

- **Impersonation**: a look-alike token claiming a real issuer.
- **Counterfeit assets**: a token with no verified issuer behind it.
- **Sanctions risk**: KYI supports screening of the issuer behind each asset.
