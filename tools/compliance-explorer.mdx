[Compliance Explorer](https://explorer.bluprynt.com) is the public face of the Bluprynt record. Search an asset, issuer or regulator and see what's verified, what's disclosed and where each fact comes from.

## What an asset page shows

| Section | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Header** | Asset type, status tags such as **Issuer on record**, and the latest reserve attestation. |
| **Disclosure checklist** | How many checklist items are on record, as a percentage, against the registry average. |
| **Listed on** | The venues trading the asset and their pairs. |
| **Chain deployments** | Every chain the asset is deployed on, with circulating supply. |
| **Tabs** | **Overview**, **Markets** and **Governance & Control** are open to everyone. **Disclosures** and **Reports** need a signed-in account. |

If an asset has no verified issuer yet, the page says so and offers **Claim this profile**. Claiming starts [KYI](/tools/kyi) in [Bluprynt Passport](/passport/verify-asset).
